Emaki · last updated 24 September 2026

Emaki is a free study tool, made by one person. These terms are short because there is not much to say.

What you are getting

A spaced repetition trainer for Japanese vocabulary, free to use, with no advertising and nothing to buy. There is no company behind it and no support desk. It is offered as it is, with no promise that it will be available, correct, or here forever.

Your progress

Progress is stored in your browser, and also with Supabase if you choose to sign in. Please do not treat either as a safe place for anything you would hate to lose. Browsers clear storage, services have outages, and a free project is not a backup service. Privacy explains what is kept and how to remove it.

Accounts

Sign in with an address you control, and do not use somebody else's. Accounts that are used to attack the service, or to abuse other people through it, can be removed.
