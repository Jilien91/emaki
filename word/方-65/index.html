方

かた

person (polite form)

Mnemonic

The same 方, read かた. Instead of pointing at a person you gesture their way, "the next one, please." Said mid karate kata: かた.

Worth knowing

Same kanji as ほう "direction", pointing politely rather than naming. As a word for a person this is the polite one. 人 ひと is neutral and 者 もの is blunter.

Built from

方
direction, person (polite)
A pictograph of a plough or banner, a thing that points a way.
