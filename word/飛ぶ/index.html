to fly

Mnemonic

It came off the fence post with no run-up at all and was over the hedge before you had the camera up. To boot, it took the sandwich: とぶ.

Worth knowing

Also jumping: 飛び込む とびこむ is to leap in. 跳ぶ is the spelling for jumping specifically.
