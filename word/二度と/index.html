二度と

にどと

never again

Mnemonic

Not a second time, not ever. The same knee に as 二 and the same 度 turn of the wheel as 一度. He is walking out of the door and telling you he will not be back: にどと.

Worth knowing

Always with a negative later in the sentence: 二度と来ません, I will never come here again.
