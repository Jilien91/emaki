判断

はんだん

judgment, decision

Mnemonic

A blade telling two things apart, and an axe taking one of them off for good. A hand はん and Dan だん between them, and neither is reversible: はんだん.

Worth knowing

判断する is to judge or to decide. 決める きめる is picking; 判断 is the weighing that comes first.

Built from

判

刂 a standing blade on the right, cutting two things apart so you can tell which is which.
