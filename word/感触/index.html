感触

かんしょく

feeling, touch

Mnemonic

What comes back through the fingers the moment they land on it. That cloth answers like silk, and the wrong one can shock かんしょく you: かんしょく.

Worth knowing

気持ち きもち, 気分 きぶん and 感じ かんじ are how you feel inside. 触る さわる is the act of touching, and 感触 is what comes back through your hand.
