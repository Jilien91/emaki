最後

さいご

last, final

Mnemonic

The same さい reaching for the extreme end, except that now it is 後, the one trailing at the back. Sigh さい, go ご: last day of the summer holiday, and you can feel it going: さいご.

Worth knowing

後 reads ご here, same as in 今後 and 午後. 最初 さいしょ is the other end of the same pair.

Built from

最
most, extreme
A hand taking the very top thing off the pile.
後
after, behind
彳 the step radical with the rest of it trailing along behind.
