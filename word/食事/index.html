食事

しょくじ

meal, dinner

Mnemonic

A lid over a heaped dish and a matter being conducted. Not the food: the event. Shock しょく, gee じ, everybody sat down at once with the phones face down: しょくじ.

Worth knowing

食事する is to have a meal. ご飯 ごはん is the everyday word for it.
