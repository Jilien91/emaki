調子

ちょうし

condition, state of health

Mnemonic

The word for how a thing is running, tuned well or tuned badly. The same 調 as 調べる and 調査, and today the body it belongs to is running badly: ちょうし.

Worth knowing

状態 じょうたい is the state a thing is in, flatly. 調子 is how well it is going, and it is the word for how you feel.
