今朝

けさ

this morning

Mnemonic

今 now and 朝 morning welded together, and then read like neither of them. Sun up, moon still hanging there, and the case you packed last night still by the door: けさ.

Worth knowing

Irregular reading, けさ, never いまあさ.

Built from

今
now
A lid drawn shut over a single stroke, this moment, closed off from the rest.
朝
morning
日 the sun caught among the strokes with 月 the moon still up beside it, the overlap at dawn.
