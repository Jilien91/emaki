一部

いちぶ

(one) part

Mnemonic

One section of it and the rest left exactly alone. The same itch いち, and a bu ぶ of it. Change that much of the plan and the plan still stands: いちぶ.

Worth knowing

部分 ぶぶん is a part in general. 一部 is one part of a thing, with the remainder still there.
