手段

しゅだん

means, way

Mnemonic

The hand you use and the step you take to get there, and he was not fussy about either of them. A shoe しゅ and Dan だん holding the door: whatever it took: しゅだん.

Worth knowing

方法 ほうほう is a method, the way a thing is done. 手段 is the means used to reach an end, often with a hint of the end justifying them.
