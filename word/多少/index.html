多少

たしょう

a little, somewhat

Mnemonic

Many and few in the one word, landing somewhere between the two. There are a few things wrong with it, the way a tash たしょう is not quite a beard: たしょう.

Worth knowing

ちょっと is the everyday a little. 多少 is stiffer and is at home in writing.
