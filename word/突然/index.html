突然

とつぜん

abruptly, suddenly

Mnemonic

He was walking beside you, and then he was forty yards up the road. No toots とつ, no warning, nothing: とつぜん.

Worth knowing

The written and formal one. いきなり is the abrupt spoken version, 急に きゅうに is a state changing fast. Shares its 然 with 全然 and 当然.

Built from

穴 hole + 犬 dog
然
