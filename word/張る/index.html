張る

はる

to strain, to be stretched

Mnemonic

Pulled tight and held there until it aches, and after a day like that it is your shoulders. Haul はる on it and it still does not give: はる.

Worth knowing

Same reading as 春 はる, spring. 引っ張る ひっぱる is the tugging one, and this is being stretched taut.
