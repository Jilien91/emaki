笑顔

えがお

smile

Mnemonic

Not the laugh itself, the face doing it, and it comes up eager えが before the joke has even landed. On a baby it finishes you: えがお.

Worth knowing

笑う わらう is the verb. 笑顔 is the face, and it's the word you'd use for a photo.
