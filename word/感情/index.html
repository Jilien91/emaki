感情

かんじょう

feeling, emotion

Mnemonic

Two hearts in the one word, which is about right for the subject. The same can かん as 感じる and the same Jo じょう as 情報: can Joe explain any of it: かんじょう.

Worth knowing

The emotion itself. 感じ かんじ is the impression you get; 気持ち きもち is nearer a physical sensation.
