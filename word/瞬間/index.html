しゅんかん

instant, moment

Mnemonic

The same Khan かん you met in 時間 checking his watch, except this time he only looks down for as long as a blink. That is all the warning you got before the cat went: しゅんかん.

Worth knowing

間 reads かん here, the same as in 時間 じかん.
