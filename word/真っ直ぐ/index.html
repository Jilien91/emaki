真っ直ぐ

まっすぐ

straight ahead, straightforward

Mnemonic

True straight, both characters saying it and neither of them bending. A mass まっす of road and not one bend in it. Down this way and keep going: まっすぐ.

Worth knowing

素直 すなお is straightforward about a person, honest and unresisting. 真っ直ぐ is a line, and it does an honest person as well.

Built from
