お菓子

おかし

sweets, snacks

Mnemonic

Grass and fruit worked into something that is not a meal and was never meant to be. Oh, cash し, look what these cost. Round to everybody: おかし.

Worth knowing

甘い あまい is sweet as a taste. お菓子 is sweets as the things you eat.

Built from

菓
confectionery
艹 grass + 果 fruit, result
子

A swaddled infant with its arms out, in three strokes.
