対応

たいおう

correspondence, equivalence, response

Mnemonic

You turn to face the thing and answer it, which is what a good one looks like. He gets back to people the same day, tie たい on, because he owes おう them that: たいおう.

Worth knowing

反応 はんのう is a reaction, what happens to you. 対応 is a response you choose and then manage.
