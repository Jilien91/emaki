不意に

ふいに

suddenly, unexpectedly

Mnemonic

Nothing in anybody’s mind said it was coming, and then she was on her feet. The same foo ふ that cancels things in 不可能, arriving with no notice at all: ふいに.

Worth knowing

急に きゅうに is suddenly as a change of pace. 不意に is suddenly as in nobody saw it coming.
