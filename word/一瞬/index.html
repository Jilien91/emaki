一瞬

いっしゅん

instant, for a moment

Mnemonic

一 one 瞬 blink, the same 瞬 that sits in 瞬間. That is precisely how long you spent not believing your own ears: いっしゅん.

Worth knowing

瞬間 しゅんかん is the moment itself; 一瞬 is 'for an instant', and behaves like an adverb.
