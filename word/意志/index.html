意志

いし

will, volition

Mnemonic

What the mind has settled on with the heart stood behind it. The same いし as 石 the stone, which is the whole point: he does not bend: いし.

Worth knowing

Same reading as 石 いし, a stone. 意思 いし written with 思 is a near neighbour, closer to intention than to resolve.

Built from

意
idea, mind
音 sound + 心 heart
志

士 retainer + 心 heart
