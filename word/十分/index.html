十分

じゅうぶん

plenty

Mnemonic

Ten, and the parts: all ten tenths of it. The same juice じゅう as 十 and the same bun ぶん as 分, money still in the account at month end and enough left to chew on the way home: じゅうぶん.

Worth knowing

Also written 充分. Careful: 十分 also reads じっぷん, meaning ten minutes, with the same two characters.
