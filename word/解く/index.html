Mnemonic

You untie the knot in the problem and the whole thing comes apart in your hands. Talk とく it through and it opens up. Can you do this one: とく.

Worth knowing

解ける とける is a problem coming undone by itself. Read ほどく the same character is untying a knot or a ribbon.
