会話

かいわ

conversation

Mnemonic

People gathering in under a roof and a tongue doing the work. Not one person talking: the part where it goes back and forth, which is the part that counts. Kai かい and wa わ, both at it: かいわ.

Worth knowing

Same 話 as 話す はなす. 会話 is the exchange itself rather than the talking.
