かんかく

sensation

Mnemonic

All of it landing on a heart, and the eye that took it in. Below thinking. Can かん you can かく say how? What your hand knows about the water before you do: かんかく.

Worth knowing

感じ かんじ is the impression; 感覚 is the faculty itself. Same 覚 as 覚える おぼえる.
