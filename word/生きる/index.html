生きる

いきる

to live, to exist

Mnemonic

Forcing your way up out of the ground, which is neither comfortable nor guaranteed. A hundred years of it is the plan regardless: いきる.

Worth knowing

生 turns up everywhere with different readings. 生 なま is raw, 先生 せんせい is a teacher.
