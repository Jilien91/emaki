移動

いどう

movement, shift

Mnemonic

Grain being shifted about in quantity, and heavy things moved by force. Not a stroll: getting an entire party and its luggage from one place to the next. Ee い, dough どう, and off we all go: いどう.

Worth knowing

移動する is moving from place to place. 引っ越す ひっこす is moving house.
