肉体

にくたい

body, flesh

Mnemonic

Meat and body, which is the body as a physical object rather than as you. A nick にく of meat and a tie たい round it, and it does not last forever: にくたい.

Worth knowing

精神 せいしん is the mind, and 肉体 is the thing it gets set against. 身体 しんたい is the body as a whole, health included, where 肉体 is the flesh of it.
