以上

いじょう

... or more, beyond

Mnemonic

A marker put down to mean starting here, and everything stacked above it. The same じょう as 異常, one mora off it: thirty degrees and up, day after day, right through the summer: いじょう.

Worth knowing

Takes in the number itself, 30度以上 includes 30. 以下 いか does the same from below.

Built from

以
by means of, from
A person with a mark set beside them, 'starting from here', used to fix a boundary.
上
up, above
A mark sitting above the line.

Also read いじょう

異常 extraordinary, exceptional
