来月

らいげつ

next month

Mnemonic

The crop not yet in, sat on the moon you could ski down the light of. The same らい as 来年: one more turn of it and you stop being a school pupil for good: らいげつ.

Worth knowing

月 reads げつ here, not つき as in 毎月 まいつき. Same 来 as 来年 らいねん.

Built from

来
come
Originally a wheat plant. The crop that comes to you at harvest.
月
moon, month
A crescent with lines drawn across it. Squeezed onto the left of a word about the body it stands for flesh instead.
