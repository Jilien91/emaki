目覚める

めざめる

to wake (up), to come to one's senses

Mnemonic

The eye comes back on and everything behind it comes on with it. The same me め as 目, opening at half six this morning: めざめる.

Worth knowing

起きる おきる is getting up out of bed. 目覚める is the waking itself, and it does coming to your senses about something.
