土曜日

どようび

Saturday

Mnemonic

Earth 土 day, which is Saturn, and English kept that one whole and unchanged. The club, that night: どようび.

Built from

土
earth, soil
A mound of earth drawn as a heap sitting on the line of the ground.
曜
day of the week
日 the sun on the left with feathers and a bird above it. It marks the seven days, one for each of the old heavenly bodies.
日
sun, day
A pictograph, the sun with a mark in the middle.
