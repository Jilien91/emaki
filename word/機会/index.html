機会

きかい

chance, opportunity, occasion

Mnemonic

The moment the parts line up and the right people happen to be in the room together. A key き, and Kai かい in the doorway. There have been a great many of those lately: きかい.

Worth knowing

場 ば on its own is the occasion rather than the chance to act on it.
